# Home (and /portfolio): company website

Scope: `/` home and `/portfolio` project list. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: corporate and established business owners in Uganda checking whether Maverick is a serious company, often on a phone. Action: book a free consultation (or call / WhatsApp) in a few taps. Proof: live client sites, open pricing from UGX 1,000,000, the founder's face and words, client quotes. Constraints: dark only (owner decision 2026-09-28, reconfirmed 2026-10-01): near-black page with real navy panels; blue, white, black brand; nothing invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A lively, confident corporate-services site in the owner's reference class (Biztop, Creatix, Finoza), executed in Maverick navy, azure and white. It refuses both the flat all-white document look the owner rejected and a one-to-one copy of the references' green and lime layouts.

OWN-WORLD: Committed dark. Near-black page (#07090c) with rounded navy panels (#0c1a2d: hero, work, fees highlight, footer) and one azure-lit navy panel for booking, the closing beat. White type; logo azure (#1a8cf0) is the single accent: the typed headline word, highlighted phrases, the arrow disc in white pill buttons, icon tiles. Rounded 20px cards, full pills for actions, glass floating nav and proof cards. Plus Jakarta Sans at medium weight (owner choice, StratEdge-style).

STORY: The visitor lands on a hero wall of real client sites with a clear promise and proof cards (starting fee, reply from Martin), sees who Maverick has built for and the work itself straight away, then the two-tone statement, services, the founder, process, clear fees, client words tied to their sites, answers, and books a time in three taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A floating glass nav pill inside the hero (logo, section links with a current-section dot, All projects, white Book pill). The hero is a rounded panel inset 12px from the viewport edges, filled with a tilted wall of the eight live client sites under a dark wash: bottom-left, a Kampala chip, a medium-weight headline whose last word types and cycles in azure (established, credible, professional, trustworthy), one sentence, the Book a free consultation pill and an outline Call pill; bottom-right, glass proof cards (starting fee; Martin replies within one business day). On phones the quick-contact bar stays hidden until the hero scrolls away. References added by the owner: StratEdge (rounded inset panels, two-tone statement).

FORM: The category standard at the owner's references' craft level; seed key ea432ce8. Signature interaction: the three-step booking slip that ends in a reference-numbered receipt (ring-and-tick draw, rows land in turn), prefilled WhatsApp and an add-to-calendar link. Motion (owner asked for a live, interactive site with typing, 2026-10-01): the hero wall comes into focus, drifts in opposing columns and leans from the pointer on desktop; headline words rise; section highlights type once on arrival; work cards rise with a screenshot wipe; the statement lights word by word on scroll; cards carry a cursor light; a client-name marquee. All of it has a reduced-motion path.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
