# Home (and /portfolio): company website

Scope: `/` home and `/portfolio` project list. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: corporate and established business owners in Uganda checking whether Maverick is a serious company, often on a phone. Action: book a free consultation (or call / WhatsApp) in a few taps. Proof: live client sites, open pricing from UGX 1,000,000, the founder's face and words, client quotes. Constraints: light base with deep navy fields; blue, white, black brand; nothing invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A lively, confident corporate-services site in the owner's reference class (Biztop, Creatix, Finoza), executed in Maverick navy, azure and white. It refuses both the flat all-white document look the owner rejected and a one-to-one copy of the references' green and lime layouts.

OWN-WORLD: Committed colour. Deep navy (#08182f) owns the hero, the work showcase, the booking band and the footer; white and a pale blue-grey carry the rest. Logo azure (#1a8cf0) is the single accent: highlighted words, pill buttons with a navy arrow disc, icon tiles, badges. Rounded 20px cards and images, full pills for actions, real photography with floating white proof cards, a slim navy contact bar above the nav. Geist 700 headlines.

STORY: The visitor lands on a navy hero with a clear promise, a photo, and proof cards (starting fee, reply time, a live client site), sees who Maverick has built for, what it does, the founder behind it, real results, clear fees and steps, client words and answers, then books a time in three taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim navy contact bar, then a white nav (logo, links, phone, azure Book pill). The hero is a rounded full-bleed photo panel (the blue laptop photo under a navy gradient) inset 12px from the viewport edges: bottom-left, a chip of stacked client-site thumbnails (8 live client websites), a 700-weight headline with its last phrase in azure, one sentence, the azure Book a free consultation pill and an outline Call pill; bottom-right, floating white proof cards (starting fee; Martin replies within one business day). References added by the owner: StratEdge (rounded inset panels, two-tone statement, stat tiles).

FORM: The category standard at the owner's references' craft level (standing exit taken after the Company Profile build was rejected as boring); seed key ea432ce8. Signature interaction: the three-step booking slip that ends in a reference-numbered receipt, prefilled WhatsApp and an add-to-calendar link; supported by floating proof cards that settle onto the hero photo once, and a client-name marquee.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
